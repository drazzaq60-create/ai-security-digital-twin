# Outdated and Vulnerable Software

Running outdated software (such as an old content management system, or CMS) means known vulnerabilities with published exploits stay open. Attackers scan the internet for these known CVEs (Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures) and exploit them automatically. An outdated, unpatched CMS is one of the most common initial-access points attackers use to get their first foothold.

How to fix: keep all software patched and up to date. Maintain an inventory of your components and their versions, subscribe to vulnerability feeds, and apply security updates promptly. Remove unused plugins, services, and accounts to shrink the attack surface.
